Our Story

Welcome to Your Himalayan Home

Nestled in the pristine valleys of Uttarakhand, Bisht Homestay offers a peaceful retreat for nature lovers and adventurers. As local residents, we know every hidden trail, majestic viewpoint, and ancient forest in the region.

Whether you're resting before the great Roopkund trek, seeking the vast meadows of Ali Bedni Bugyal, or simply looking to escape city life with authentic local food and warm hospitality — our doors are always open. Experience the mountains not as a tourist, but as part of our family.
